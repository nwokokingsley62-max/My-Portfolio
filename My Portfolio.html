Case Studies

Click any project to see the thinking behind the pixels.

01

Skills & Opportunity MarketplaceConnecting talent with opportunity — UX case study

✦

Problem: Job seekers struggled to find relevant opportunities, and businesses couldn't reach verified skilled talent efficiently.

Approach: Conducted user interviews and competitive analysis, mapped user journeys, then designed wireframes → hi-fi prototypes with a component-based design system.

User ResearchJourney MappingHi-Fi Prototyping

Outcome: A clear, searchable marketplace flow reducing time-to-apply and improving listing quality.

02

CarBreezy Car MarketplaceReimagining how people buy cars online

✦

Problem: Car listings felt cluttered and comparison was painful — users dropped off before contacting sellers.

Approach: Simplified information architecture, designed a card-based browsing system with smart filters and a side-by-side comparison flow.

IAFiltering UXResponsive UI

Outcome: Cleaner browsing, faster decisions, and a listing flow sellers complete in minutes.

03

ROYAN Hospital WebsiteHealthcare UX built on trust and accessibility

✦

Problem: Patients couldn't quickly find doctors, services or booking info — the old site hurt credibility.

Approach: Designed a calm, accessible interface with clear service categories, doctor profiles and a simple appointment journey. WCAG-conscious color and type choices throughout.

AccessibilityPatient JourneysHTML/CSS Build

Outcome: A trustworthy digital front door that makes care easier to find and book.

04

Pivot Bar & Bistro RedesignA brand with personality, served fresh

✦

Problem: The brand's online presence didn't match its in-person energy — static, dated and forgettable.

Approach: Created a bold visual direction with rich imagery, immersive menu design, events showcase and a friction-free reservation flow.

Visual DesignBrand UIReservations UX

Outcome: A site that finally tastes as good as the food.
